Ventures

In this agentic-AI world, research and entrepreneurship must walk hand in hand. Research without entrepreneurship stays on paper and cannot solve real problems. Entrepreneurship without research becomes a hotch-potch — a stitching-together of existing solutions that will not advance the field or help real users at scale. My work lives at the meeting point: the mathematical rigour of two decades of research, carried all the way into products people actually use.

Two ventures, one conviction: deep research should end in something people can use.

Primus Credence

Founder & CEO · Dubai, UAE · primuscredence.com ↗

Provable security for applications — who can reach what inside your application, proved, not assumed.

Primus Credence brings mathematical rigour to security at the application layer, its plugins, and the seams between them — the part of the stack that sits beyond the already-proven cloud base. Detection shows what is happening; a proof shows what cannot happen.

Access control

Who can reach what — across application roles, tokens, plugin routes and users.

Network isolation

What traffic can reach where — derived from configuration, without sending a single packet.

Data residency

Where regulated data could go — including backups, replicas, keys and caches.

  • Every engagement ends in either a proof or a concrete counter-example — the exact thing that breaks it — followed by a fix and a re-proof.
  • Built for Platform and AppSec teams, ISVs, enterprises and government across the UAE and GCC, with MSSPs, consultancies and auditors as channel partners.
  • Research roots: PhD work on verifying information-flow security at IISc, and scalable static security analysis at Oracle Labs (six US patents) — now productised.

Nucleus Tech Works

CEO · Mysuru, India · nucleustechworks.com ↗

At Thought Speed — making high-end legal intelligence affordable and usable, from a citizen holding a notice to an advocate preparing for court.

MY Legal Desktop

MY Legal Desktop is an agentic-AI legal workspace built for Indian law as it stands today — drafting, document review, evidentiary gap analysis and case-strength assessment — for citizens, advocates, law firms, corporate legal teams and government bodies. Open MY Legal Desktop ↗

What’s new in it

Statute-anchored reasoning engine

Legal reasoning is grounded in the statutory text itself, not in a language model’s recollection of the law. Every conclusion traces back to the provision it rests on, so it can be checked rather than trusted.

Time-aware law

Answers reflect the law as it stood on the date of the matter. As Indian criminal and procedural law is re-codified, the provision that applies today may not be the one that applied when the events occurred.

Evidentiary gap analysis & case strength

It goes beyond drafting: it reasons about what a case still needs to prove, which evidence is missing, and how strong the matter is — analysis that normally demands a senior practitioner.

MY Virtual Attorney — adapts to its user

One counsel layer, two very different users: plain-language guidance for a citizen, citation-backed analysis for an advocate. It drafts FIRs, petitions and consumer complaints, and assists with court preparation.

Vernacular-first by design

Speech in, speech out, and photograph-a-notice OCR, with full Kannada support, built on AI4Bharat’s Indic speech and OCR models — so legal help reaches people who do not read or type English.

Human decides, by construction

Every output is a proposal to accept, edit or discard. Nothing leaves the desk without a qualified person’s approval, and AI-generated content is always labelled — never passed off as official wording.

Safety-first citizen pathway

A guided, non-diagnostic checklist routes citizens to the right help, and surfaces safety resources immediately when there is any sign of danger.

Measured on real usage, not benchmarks

Every accept, edit and reject is instrumented, so the system is measured and corrected against how practitioners actually use it — not against leaderboard scores.

Research roots: two decades of formal methods, program analysis and information-flow verification. The same discipline that proves properties of programs here grounds legal reasoning in statute and keeps every step auditable.

Research → Product → Real users at scale. That pipeline is what I build.
